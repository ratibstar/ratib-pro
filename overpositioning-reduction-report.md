# Over-positioning reduction report

**Task:** Reduce startup-pretending-to-be-sovereign-infrastructure and Palantir-style cosplay without removing enterprise architecture story.

---

## Categories of over-positioning found

### 1. Infrastructure cosplay
Repeated **control plane**, **production surface**, **layered control plane** across home, profile, architecture, procurement, and diagrams — suggested a national orchestration tier rather than a B2B SaaS platform.

**Reduction:** Prefer **platform layers**, **platform core**, **agency workspace**, **enterprise workspace**.

### 2. Intelligence / defense vocabulary
**Telemetry intelligence**, **intelligence at ingest**, **threat fusion**, **escape prediction**, **anti-spoof** (in marketing flows), **rate intelligence** (mega nav).

**Reduction:** **Field operations**, **signal validation**, **operational visibility**, **signal check**, **rate limiting**.

### 3. Sovereign / government authority
**Sovereign-grade**, **sovereign workforce programs**, **Government execution mode**, **government-tech** tone in eyebrows.

**Reduction:** **Regulated programs**, **policy & oversight**, **strict policy profile**, **labor-oversight tools**.

### 4. Unearned operational proof
Hard metrics (2.8k, 94.6%, 412, 238ms), **99.9% edge target**, live dash chrome (“prod”, “Live”, “cp-me-01a”).

**Reduction:** Sample labels, illustrative disclaimers, **ws-demo-01**, **Sample** badge on dash.

### 5. Buzzword stacking
Single paragraphs containing orchestration + telemetry + governance + finance-grade + multi-tenant.

**Reduction:** Shorter sentences; one primary concept per clause in home hero, platform sub, footer brand.

- Event-driven workflows, webhooks, idempotency
- Governance, RBAC, labor oversight modules

## Repetition budget (guideline for future edits)

Use at most **once per page** unless technically necessary:

- orchestration
- multi-tenant / multi-agency
- governance
- telemetry → prefer **field operations**
- infrastructure

Synonym rotation: **workflow platform**, **program workspace**, **operational visibility**, **policy controls**.
